      Ideas

      From Hearts of Iron 4 Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.

      All countries have the opportunity to pick ideas using political power. These ideas give various bonuses, and are split between several categories:

      • Conscription law: affects how much manpower is available to the country for military service, factory output, dockyard output, construction speed, and training time
      • Trade law: affects resources to market, factory output, dockyard output, construction speed, and research time
      • Economy law: affects how many factories are dedicated to consumer goods, manpower availability, civilian and military factory construction speed, and conversion speed for factories
      • Political advisors: give a variety of bonuses, and can be used to change the political environment in a country
      • Research and production companies: give reductions to research time and bonuses to equipment researched under their lead
      • Military staff: give bonuses to armies, navies, and air forces

      Conscription laws[edit | edit source]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.

      The conscription law of a country mainly determines the available Manpower for the armed forces. It can be changed for 150 Political Power per level of difference between the old and new laws if the prerequisites are met, though with No Step Back, fascist countries can get a sizable discount through the  State serves the Military officer corps spirit.

      Besides the Political Power that needs to be spent, it is fairly safe to increase conscription laws until Extensive Conscription. Starting with Service by Requirement, a country begins to experience some economic penalties that get worse with further conscription laws.

      Volunteer Only is the default conscription law and the starting one for most countries. However, many countries start on different laws like Limited Conscription or Disarmed Nation instead, including  Germany,  France,  Italy, the  United States, and  Japan.

      Trade laws[edit | edit source]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.

      The trade law of a country determines how many resources of the controlled provinces are exported. Only the remaining part of the resources can be used for production or trade. Other nations can trade the exported resources versus Civilian factories.

      The more free trade is, the greater the benefits for Factory output,  Construction Speed and  Research Speed. A country's trade law can be changed for 150 Political Power if the prerequisites are met.

      Export Focus is the default trade law and the starting one for most countries. However, some countries start with Limited Exports or Free Trade instead, including  Germany,  Italy, the  United States,  China, and  Japan.  Communist China is the only country that starts with Closed Economy.

      Economy laws[edit | edit source]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.

      The economy law of a country defines how many  civilian factories of all factories are needed to produce  Consumer Goods, the  Construction Speed and conversion costs of factories, and the efficiency of Fuel production. It can be changed for 150  political power if the prerequisites are met.

      Higher tiers of economy law are generally more efficient than lower ones, especially when constructing factories. Although Total Mobilization reduces the amount of recruitable population available for Manpower, the effect can be counteracted with the Women in the Workforce decision that becomes available for 100 Political Power (though it will need to be retaken should the country no longer be at war).

      Civilian Economy is the default economy law and the starting one for most countries. Some countries start on Partial Mobilization, however, including  Germany,  Italy,  Japan, and  Communist China.

      Design companies[edit | edit source]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.
      See also: List of design companies
      See also: Military Industrial Organizations

      Design companies provide bonuses to researched equipment. A country can select:

      • one each of tank, naval, air, and materiel designers.
        • With Arms Against Tyranny, these are not available and are instead replaced with military industrial organizations.
      • one industrial concern.

      Bonuses from the tank, naval, and air design companies are only applied to equipment designed while the design company was hired. For example, if a tank design company is hired 1 day before a tank is designed, that tank will receive the design company's bonuses permanently. (However, it may be, in some cases, that which bonus is applied is instead determined by which design company was active on the in-game day when the research unlocking the chassis was completed.) If a design company is replaced with another, only the new design company's bonuses will be applied to any equipment designed or researched. A design company's bonuses will never be applied to any equipment that was fully researched prior to hiring the design company. (Note that this is different from national spirits which modify production cost: those are applied to all equipment produced while they are active, regardless of when it was researched or designed.) Design company bonuses are also applied to variants.

      This can be manipulated to maximize useful bonuses by designing tanks, ships, and airplanes at the time when the design company which gives the best bonuses for that type of equipment is hired.

      Military chiefs[edit | edit source]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.

In addition to their specific effects, Chiefs of Army provide daily Army experience, Chiefs of Navy provide daily Navy experience, and Chiefs of Airforce provide daily Air experience. Specialists provide +0.2 daily experience, Experts provide +0.3 daily experience, and Geniuses provide +0.4 daily experience.

      Military high command[edit | edit source]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.
      See also: List of military high command

      Up to three members of the military high command may be chosen for Military High Command positions, giving global bonuses to their branch of service.

      Some military high command advisers give bonuses to particular types of combat battalions. Their effects are weighed by the ratio of the target battalion count in the combat battalions of the target division. The attack modifier will be applied on both soft and hard attack regardless of being the attacker or the defender, and the defense modifier will be applied on defense and breakthrough depending on being the attacker or the defender. In some cases two advisers may offer the same type of bonus, and these are additive.
