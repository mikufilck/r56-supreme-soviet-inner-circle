      Rules[edit | edit source]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.

      A country may only create a new faction if the respective country rule is enabled. At the start of the game the three existing faction leaders  Germany, the  United Kingdom and the  Soviet Union, along with the  United States, have this rule. Other nations gain the capability to form their own factions through their national focus trees. The only exception is the British Raj with Together for Victory Enabled. Joining a faction is again limited by country rules. Depending on ideology and national spirits, countries need various amounts of world tension before they can join or ask someone to join a faction.

      Faction members share military access with one another and see accurate intelligence in the diplomacy screen of their allies.

      Factions rules can amend some country rules such as the ability to justify war goals on other countries of the same ideology or which actions are allowed in peace conferences.

      Faction members can choose to leave a faction, but this causes a significant relations modifier faction traitor (-75 relations). A faction member cannot leave while fighting a war on the side of other members. The faction leader may kick another country out even while at war or disband the faction altogether if it contains no other members than themselves and their subjects. If the faction leader ceases to exist, the largest remaining member takes over.

      If a country fights alongside major allies, it will only surrender once all of the majors in the faction have capitulated. For instance if Poland joined the Allies, it will not surrender before the United Kingdom and France capitulate, and will instead continue on as a government-in-exile.

      Faction leader[edit | edit source]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.

      A faction leader in HOI4 is the leader of a faction. The default faction leaders in-game are the  United Kingdom leading the Allies,  German Reich leading the Axis and the  Soviet Union leading the Comintern. They are usually joined by  Japan leading the Greater East Asian Co-Prosperity Sphere, and  China leading the Chinese United Front.

      If the faction leader ceases to exist, the largest remaining member takes over.

      Diplomatic Actions[edit | edit source]

      The faction leader may kick another country out even while at war or disband the faction altogether if it contains no other members than themselves and their subjects.

      Assuming Leadership[edit | edit source]

      Available only with the Man the Guns DLC enabled.

      To be able to take over leadership in the faction they are in, a country must:

      • Have more than twice the deployed manpower of the faction leader (subjects count, but at varying strength depending on autonomy)
      • Have more than 150% of the factories of the faction leader
      • Be in every war the faction leader is currently in
      • Not having capitulated already

      After paying 200 Political Power, the country can become faction leader, with all the benefits it entails.

      Chinese United Front[edit | edit source]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.

      The Chinese United Front is a smaller faction that may be formed by Nationalist China in order to unite the Chinese against the Japanese invaders. Note that it does not exist in the 1936 start date. The United Front will be formed by  China, which will annex the Warlords states (except  Sinkiang) and  People's Republic of China will annex  Shanxi. With Waking the Tiger, the Chinese United front can be formed by  China with the focus 'United Front' and the Warlord cliques ( Shanxi,  Xibei San Ma,  Yunnan,  Guangxi Clique  Sinkiang) and  Communist China all then will join. Without the DLC, the faction is formed via events. By completing the focus 'One China Policy'  China leaves the faction.

      Greater East Asia Co-Prosperity Sphere[edit | edit source]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.

      The Greater East Asia Co-Prosperity Sphere (formerly known as Daitoa Kyoeiken) is a faction formed by  Japan by doing the focus of the same name. It's formed with the objective of creating a Pan-Asian bloc led by Japan. China or any Chinese Warlord can join if they have a Non-Aggression Pact (NAP) with Japan and is at war with China or Shanxi after the Marco Polo Bridge Incident Event fires and China choose "We have reached the breaking point!" Decision. Any nation, such as Finland, if guarantee by faction member will join the faction if attacked. Japan and GEACPS members will fight a defensive war against invading nation, such as Soviet Union.

      Other possible factions[edit | edit source]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.

      All countries (except the  British Raj with Together for Victory) can acquire the ability to create factions through their national focus tree. In general, these have no default name, with the player choosing a name when inviting the first member. Several specific factions, inspired by counterfactual history, do have pre-set names, however, and are listed below.

      Międzymorze[edit | edit source]

      Active with Poland's unique focus tree available through the free DLC with the 'The Between the Seas Concept' focus. It will be formed by  Poland and can be expanded to include the Baltic states. The faction has bases on the historical idea of the Intermarium, in Polish Międzymorze, a plan created by politician Józef Piłsudski in which the Eastern European countries that broke free from Russian control would create a federation between the Baltic and Black seas. In the game, however, the Międzymorze is only a military alliance.

      Treaty of Reciprocal Assistance[edit | edit source]

      If the  United States takes the focus 'Hemisphere Defense', they will form this faction and automatically invite all Latin American nations of its ideology into it. Note that the nations invited will differ depending on what ideology the US is at the time it completes the focus.

      The faction itself is likely based on the real-life Inter-American Treaty of Reciprocal Assistance, a treaty that was signed after World War 2. Unlike the real-life treaty, which merely invited countries based on their locations, the in-game faction will only invite Latin American countries of the same ideology as the US, meaning that  Venezuela,  Argentina,  Paraguay,  Brazil,  Bolivia,  Peru,  Dominican Republic,  El Salvador, and  Mexico will not be invited if a Democratic US was to form the faction, despite having signed the treaty in the first year (although should a Fascist or Communist US be the one to form the faction, the countries belonging to those ideologies would be invited instead).

       Finland can create this faction via the focus 'Finnish Supremacy in the North' after flipping to a fascist regime. It will invite any independent fascist Nordic or Baltic country and boost fascism in any other one.

      Possible members
      •  Finland (automatic)

      Norden League[edit | edit source]

       Norway can create this faction via the focus 'Shield of the North' after following the monarchist path and reforming the Kingdom of Norway. After that the nation can claim  Iceland as well as parts of  Denmark,  Sweden and the  Soviet Union.

      Possible members
      •  Norway (automatic)

      Nordic Cooperation Alliance[edit | edit source]

       Denmark can form this faction by reaffirming neutrality and starting the rearmament. After completing the focus 'Strength in Numbers' the nation can either join one of the three major powers or create its own faction - all via decision.

      Possible members
      •  Denmark (automatic)
      •  Iceland (automatic)
      •  Sweden
      •  Norway
      •  Finland
      •  France

      Nordic Council[edit | edit source]

       Norway can create this faction after holding a vote of no confidence and bringing Johan H. Andresen to power. Completing the focus 'Proactive Defense' creates the Nordic Council. Further down the focus path  Norway can get Annex war goals against  Sweden and  Denmark as well as a Topple Government war goal against the  German Reich. Norway can afterwards still join the Axis, which will dismantle the Nordic Council.

      Possible members
      •  Norway (automatic)

      Norintern[edit | edit source]

       Norway can create this faction with the focus 'Reawaken the Permanent Revolution' after flipping to communism and deciding to prosecute the NKP.

      Possible members
      •  Norway (automatic)
      •  Sinkiang
      •  Paraguay
      •  Communist China

      Northern Defense Front [democratic][edit | edit source]

       Finland can create this faction in two ways. This second - democratic - way is only available with the Arms Against Tyranny DLC. The nation has to choose Social-Democracy after concentrating on liberating the working class. After neglecting joining he Comintern or the Allies  Finland has to complete the focus 'United Under the North Star' after forming a union with  Estonia and will then be able to invite the Nordic states.

      Possible members
      •  Finland (automatic)
      •  Estonia
      •  Sweden
      •  Norway
      •  Denmark

      Northern National Front[edit | edit source]

       Denmark can create this faction after flipping to fascism and deciding against German support. The newly fascist nation sways the public opinion of the Nordic countries and offers them protection, eventually annexing them.

      Possible members
      •  Denmark (automatic)
      •  Norway
      •  Sweden
      •  Finland
      •  Iceland
      •  Estonia
      •  Latvia
      •  Lithuania
      •  Poland

      Northern People's Union[edit | edit source]

       Finland can create this faction via concentrating on liberating the working class and tending towards a red government. After flipping to communism and forming a union with  Estonia  Finland can take the focus 'United Under the North Star' to create the Northern People's Union and invite the Nordic countries.

      Possible members
      •  Finland (automatic)
      •  Estonia
      •  Sweden
      •  Norway
      •  Denmark

      Northern Security Pact[edit | edit source]

       Denmark can form this faction after unifying the Right and letting King Christian X. assume power. Through the focus 'Scandinavian Security' the Kingdom of  Denmark can then decide to create its own faction and invite the Nordic countries. In the end  Denmark will reintegrate  Iceland and completely annex all Nordics.

      Possible members
      •  Denmark (automatic)
      •  Iceland (automatic)
      •  Sweden
      •  Norway
      •  Finland

      Scandinavian Defense Union[edit | edit source]

       Sweden can form this faction with the focus of the same name after flipping to communism, liquidating the monarchy and supporting the red ideology in the Nordic countries.

      Possible members
      •  Sweden (automatic)
      •  Norway
      •  Denmark

      Swedish-Finnish Defense Agreement[edit | edit source]

       Sweden can form this faction with the focus of the same name after either going down the fascist or monarchist path. The country can afterwards still join the Allies, which will dismantle the Defense Agreement.

      Possible members
      •  Sweden (automatic)
      •  Finland

      The Nordic Defense Council[edit | edit source]

       Sweden can form this faction with the focus 'Decentralized Nordic Army Command' after concentrating on the  Soviet Union as the old enemy.

      Possible members
      •  Sweden (automatic)
      •  Norway
      •  Denmark
      •  Finland
